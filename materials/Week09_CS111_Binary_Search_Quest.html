👩‍🏫 Classroom instructions
1. Ask students to predict LOW, HIGH and MID.
2. Let them click the midpoint tile to earn XP.
3. Press Next Step and explain the if / else decision.
4. Notice the excluded range is still in memory.
5. Compare with linear search and discuss O(log n) versus O(n) worst-case time.
